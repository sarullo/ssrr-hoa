About the HOA

Established 1955 · Rancho Palos Verdes, California

A Community Built on the Ridge

The Sunnyside Community Association was established in 1955 when developer James Cornell built and incorporated homes on Sunnyside Ridge Road. For over 70 years, our association has been the backbone of this unique hilltop community.

Currently, the Association has a membership of more than 50 families who own homes on Sunnyside Ridge, Sol Vista, and Palos Verdes Drive East.

Unlike many other residential streets on the peninsula, Sunnyside Ridge is not a private road. Because early homeowners granted the right-of-way to the City of Rancho Palos Verdes, street maintenance is funded and coordinated by the City.

The Association is committed to properly maintaining the entrance and neighborhood, in addition to ensuring the well-being of all residents through emergency preparedness and networking activities.

🚔 Lomita Sheriff's Department

The HOA posts all crime information in our area as published by the Lomita Sheriff's Department. Check regularly to stay aware of recent crime activity.

You can also listen to a South Bay Police live audio feed in real time for up-to-the-minute public safety information.

👁 What is Neighborhood Watch?

As a member of an organized Neighborhood Watch, our street is part of a citywide crime prevention network connecting Neighborhood Watch, the Sheriff's Department, and Block Captains.

How it works:

  • Crime warnings are emailed to Block Captains
  • Block Captains disseminate alerts to Block Assistants
  • Alerts are shared with all participating Neighborhood Watch residents
